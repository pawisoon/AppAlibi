# Changelog

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-12)

AppAlibi now keeps a separate record of what AI assistants and coding agents do on your Mac: Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex, Copilot, Gemini, Kimi, Cursor, Perplexity and a few others.

- New AI tab. Each client gets a card with what macOS was seen allowing it (screen, input, mouse and keyboard control, Full Disk Access, admin rights) and a timeline of what it used and when. A screen recording that comes with mouse and keyboard control is marked as probable computer use.
- Apps or command-line tools AppAlibi doesn't know can be added in Settings, under AI Clients.
- The Permissions tab shows which apps were seen with Full Disk Access or control of the Mac.
- Two new notifications, off by default: one for AI clients, one for the first time an app gets Full Disk Access. The welcome tour turns on the AI one along with the others.
- The Summary tab has an AI Clients section.
- Settings is split into tabs instead of one long page.
- Switching between history tabs is faster, and each tab keeps its filters.
- "Keyboard Control" is now "Mouse & Keyboard Control", since that permission covers both.

History recorded before 0.3.0 is copied into the AI tab once. Older records only have daily totals, so those rows are marked approximate.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-10)
