AppAlibi

Every app needs an alibi. So does every AI agent.

AI agents read your files, record your screen and move your mouse, in seconds and often while you're looking elsewhere. AppAlibi keeps a record of what they did, and what every other app on your Mac did too.

Free and open source. macOS 15 or later.

What is AppAlibi?

A Mac app that keeps a history of which apps and AI agents used your camera, microphone and screen, controlled your Mac, or opened your files.

Why Now?

macOS asks you once, then stays quiet. An agent you allowed last week can take over the mouse today, and the only sign is a dot that's gone a second later.

Why Trust AppAlibi?

It's fully open source, has no external dependencies, and makes no network requests. Your history never leaves your Mac.

See What Your AI Agents Did

AppAlibi recognizes AI assistants and coding agents, including the command-line tools that run inside Terminal, and keeps a timeline for each one.

And Every Other App

The green and orange dots tell you what's happening right now. AppAlibi remembers what happened before, so you can look back at the day or the week.
