What is AppAlibi?

A Mac menu bar app that keeps a history of which apps used your camera, microphone and screen, and which ones asked for your files.

Why Bother?

The green and orange dots show you what's happening right now. Once they go away, macOS keeps no record you can look back at. AppAlibi does.

Why Trust AppAlibi?

It's fully open source, has no external dependencies, and makes no network requests. Your history stays on your Mac.

What AppAlibi Keeps Track Of

Camera, Mic
and Screen

Every session, with the app, when it started and how long it ran. Location too, if you want it.

Your
Files

Apps that asked for Documents, Desktop, Downloads or Full Disk Access, and whether macOS let them in.

AI
Agents

What Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Copilot, Cursor and other AI clients were allowed to do, including mouse and keyboard control.

While You Were Away

Get told when you unlock your Mac if something used the camera, mic or screen while it was locked.

Admin Rights

See when macOS gave an app administrator rights, and get a notification when it happens.

Summaries

Daily and weekly summaries of which apps used what, and for how long.

CSV Export

Export any filtered history to a spreadsheet.

Menu Bar or Dock

Keep it out of the way in the menu bar, or give it a Dock icon.

English and Polish

Fully translated, with Apple's own Polish terms.
